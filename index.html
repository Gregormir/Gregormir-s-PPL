Push Day Mechanics
Flat DB Bench Press

Keep your shoulder blades pinched tightly into the bench. Retract and depress your scapula to shield your rotator cuffs, and maintain a subtle arch in your lower back for stable leg drive.

Arnold Press

Begin with palms completely facing you at chin level. Rotate smoothly as you drive upward, finishing the press with palms facing out. Avoid letting your elbows flare wide out at the bottom to protect the anterior delts.

Dips

Lean your torso forward slightly to isolate chest engagement. Keep your elbows tucked near your ribs rather than flaring sideways. Target a clean 90-degree break at the elbow structure before pressing back up.

DB Lateral Raises

Lean your upper body slightly forward. Control the weights with your elbows guiding the upward path. Keep your hands relaxed to reduce forearm tension, and avoid shrugging the traps at peak contraction.

DB Skullcrushers

Angle your upper arms back slightly (roughly 15 degrees behind your head) instead of perfectly perpendicular to the ceiling. This keeps constant, uninterrupted tension running directly through the triceps long head.

Pull Day Mechanics
Single-Arm DB Row

Drive the movement back with your elbow toward your hip, avoiding an up-and-down sawing pattern. Keep your shoulder parallel to the floor at all times, and prevent twisting your spine at peak extension.

DB Pullovers

Lie flat on the bench to maximize core stability. Maintain a fixed, slight bend in your elbows throughout the arc. Focus strictly on pulling from your armpits to force the lats to initiate the contractive load.

DB Rear Delt Flyes

Hinge deep at your hips so your torso sits nearly parallel with the ground. Lead the raise with your pinky fingers pointing up. Focus on expanding wide to the sides, minimizing mid-back shoulder blade squeezing.

DB Bicep Curls

Pin your elbows firmly against your ribs. Suppinate your wrist (rotate your palm upward toward the ceiling) completely as you ascend, and squeeze the target apex hard without letting your shoulders swing forward.

DB Hammer Curls

Keep your hands fixed in a neutral palms-in position. This target builds the brachialis and brachioradialis muscles. Control the eccentric phase completely; avoid bouncing the dumbbells at the bottom of the rep loop.

Leg Day Mechanics
Conventional Barbell Deadlift

Set up with the bar slicing perfectly over mid-foot. Drop your hips back until shins make direct contact with the steel. Brace your abdominal wall hard, lock your lats back, and push through your feet to break ground.

Barbell Zercher Squat

Cradle the barbell deeply within the crook of your elbows. Clasp your hands tightly together. Brace your core against the front-loaded placement to stay completely upright, preventing hip flare or spinal collapse.

Bulgarian Split Squats

Step your working leg forward, resting your rear shoelaces flat on an elevated bench. Drop your weight down and slightly back. Keep your front knee tracing straight over your pinky toe to maintain knee joint tracking safety.

DB Leg Curls

Secure a dumbbell tightly between your arches while lying face down on a flat bench. Keep your hips pinned flat against the padding to avoid hip hiking. Squeeze your hamstrings at the top of the range.

Standing Calf Raises

Execute each movement from an elevated ledge to unlock a deep stretch at the heel drop. Hold the bottom extension for 1 full second to kill elastic tendon momentum, then explode up onto your big toes.
