Warm-Up Protocols
Dynamic Range of Motion

Avoid static stretching prior to heavy loading. Use arm circles, bodyweight lunges, and light band pull-aparts.

Pyramid Warm-Up Scheme

Execute progressive warm-up sets: 50% of working weight for 5 reps, 70% for 3 reps, and 90% for 1 single clean rep.

Push Day Mechanics
Flat DB Bench Press

Keep your shoulder blades pinched tightly into the bench. Retract and depress your scapula to shield your rotator cuffs.

Arnold Press

Begin with palms completely facing you at chin level. Rotate smoothly as you drive upward, finishing the press with palms facing out.

Dips

Lean your torso forward slightly to isolate chest engagement. Keep your elbows tucked near your ribs rather than flaring sideways.

DB Lateral Raises

Lean your upper body slightly forward. Control the weights with your elbows guiding the upward path.

DB Skullcrushers

Angle your upper arms back slightly (roughly 15 degrees behind your head) instead of perfectly perpendicular to the ceiling.

Pull Day Mechanics
Single-Arm DB Row

Drive the movement back with your elbow toward your hip, avoiding an up-and-down sawing pattern.

DB Pullovers

Lie flat on the bench to maximize core stability. Maintain a fixed, slight bend in your elbows throughout the arc.

DB Rear Delt Flyes

Hinge deep at your hips so your torso sits nearly parallel with the ground. Lead the raise with your pinky fingers pointing up.

DB Bicep Curls

Pin your elbows firmly against your ribs. Suppinate your wrist completely as you ascend, and squeeze the target apex hard.

DB Hammer Curls

Keep your hands fixed in a neutral palms-in position. Control the eccentric phase completely.

Leg Day Mechanics
Conventional Barbell Deadlift

Set up with the bar slicing perfectly over mid-foot. Drop your hips back until shins make direct contact with the steel.

Barbell Zercher Squat

Cradle the barbell deeply within the crook of your elbows. Clasp your hands tightly together. Brace your core hard.

Bulgarian Split Squats

Step your working leg forward, resting your rear shoelaces flat on an elevated bench. Drop your weight down and slightly back.

DB Leg Curls

Secure a dumbbell tightly between your arches while lying face down on a flat bench. Keep your hips pinned flat.

Standing Calf Raises

Execute each movement from an elevated ledge to unlock a deep stretch at the heel drop. Hold the bottom extension for 1 second.

Cardio Optimization
Zone 2 Aerobic Base building

Maintain a pace where you can comfortably sustain a conversation without gasping.

Pacing and Cadence Safety

Aim for shorter, quick strides over over-striding. Lands mid-foot underneath your center of gravity.
